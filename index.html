How to play

Steer: drag left/right, or ←/→ and A/D.
Tuck: keep a finger on the screen for a moment (you can still steer), or hold ↓, S or Space. Faster, but you turn less sharply.
The hill gets steeper the further you go. Turning hard scrubs off speed.
One crash ends the run. Trees, igloos, polar bears, snowmen, penguins and giant rolling snowballs all count.
Chasms: hit a kicker in front of one at speed to jump it. Miss the kicker or come in slow and you're in. Red posts mark where there's no kicker.
Tricks: in the air, tap ↑ for a front flip, ↓ for a back flip, ← to spin left or → to spin right (hold ←/→ to steer, ↓ to tuck), or flick the screen. Finish before you land or you crash. Chain tricks in one jump for 1x, 2x, 3x…
Score: 1 per metre, 10 per coin, 50 per chasm cleared, plus airtime and trick bonuses.
Booster pads speed you up. Ice is fast but slippery. Birds up high slow you down.
Sound: M or the speaker button to mute.
